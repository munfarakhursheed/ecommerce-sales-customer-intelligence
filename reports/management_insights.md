# Management Insights — NovaCart Retail

## Data and scope
This portfolio project uses synthetic transactional data for a fictional online retailer. It is not actual company data. The sample contains 6,500 orders, 1,200 customers, 180 products and 65 sellers, covering 2024–2025.

## Demonstration KPI snapshot
- Delivered orders: 5,685
- Gross revenue: R$ 4,458,321.05
- Indicative gross profit: R$ 2,059,055.76
- Average order value: R$ 784.23
- On-time delivery rate: 66.79%
- Average delivery time: 8.10 days
- Repeat-customer rate among customers with delivered orders: 96.05%
- Average review score: 3.99/5

## Analytical interpretation
These figures are generated from the included synthetic dataset and are reproducible. They should be treated as a demonstration of how a BI analyst would structure an executive scorecard, not as evidence about a real business.

## Recommended management actions
1. Review category-level revenue and contribution margin together before allocating promotional spend.
2. Monitor repeat purchasing by cohort and customer segment; design retention tests rather than assuming all repeat behaviour is caused by campaigns.
3. Track on-time delivery by region and seller, and investigate persistent outliers before changing logistics partners.
4. Evaluate discount campaigns using incremental contribution, not revenue alone.
5. Add marketing spend, returns cost, taxes and overhead to move from indicative gross profit to a fuller profitability measure.

## Definitions and limitations
- Gross revenue = quantity × item selling price for delivered orders.
- Indicative gross profit = gross revenue − product cost − item-level freight.
- Average order value = delivered item revenue / distinct delivered orders.
- Repeat customer rate = customers with more than one delivered order / customers with at least one delivered order.
- No marketing attribution, customer acquisition cost, overhead, tax or refund accounting is modelled.
